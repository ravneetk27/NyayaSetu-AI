POSH ACT AND POCSO ACT DETAILED INCIDENT CATEGORIES AND CLASSIFICATION GUIDE

1. POSH ACT (WORKPLACE SEXUAL HARASSMENT) CATEGORIES
Under Section 2(n) and Section 3 of the Protection of Women from Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH) Act, 2013.

A. Quid Pro Quo Harassment
Granting or withholding workplace benefits based on compliance with sexual demands.
- Implicit/Explicit Promises: Promising promotions, salary hikes, bonus, favorable performance ratings, or career opportunities in exchange for sexual favors.
- Threats to Employment: Threatening termination, bad appraisal reviews, demotion, job loss, or transfer if sexual advances are rejected.

B. Hostile Work Environment
Unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, offensive, hostile, or oppressive atmosphere at work.
- Verbal Harassment: Sexually colored remarks, inappropriate jokes, intrusive questions about personal or sexual life, unwanted romantic proposals, or explicit comments on physical appearance.
- Non-Verbal / Visual Harassment: Staring, leering, inappropriate gestures, displaying or emailing pornographic materials, sexually suggestive screensavers, wallpapers, or memes.
- Physical Harassment: Unwanted physical contact, touching, invading personal space, blocking movement, uninvited cornering, or physical advances.
- Digital / Hybrid Harassment: Sending inappropriate WhatsApp messages, late-night non-work calls, unwanted social media interactions, or sexual harassment via Slack, Teams, email, or video calls.

C. Extended Workplace Scenarios
- Off-Site / Remote Work: Incidents occurring during work travel, client meetings, corporate retreats, offsite events, official dinners, or company-provided transport.
- Third-Party Involvements: Harassment involving vendors, interns, contractors, consultants, service providers, or clients.


2. POCSO ACT (PROTECTION OF CHILDREN FROM SEXUAL OFFENCES) CATEGORIES
Under Sections 3 to 15 of the POCSO Act, 2012 (Applies to anyone under 18 years of age).

A. Physical Offences
- Penetrative Sexual Assault (Sections 3 & 4): Non-consensual penetration of any body part or object into the child's body.
- Non-Penetrative Sexual Assault (Sections 7 & 8): Touching private body parts of a child with sexual intent, or compelling a child to touch another person's body without penetration.
- Aggravated Forms (Sections 5, 6, 9, 10): Offences committed by individuals in positions of trust or authority (e.g., teachers, family members, doctors, police officers, caretakers, school staff) or resulting in bodily harm, injury, or pregnancy.

B. Non-Physical & Digital Offences
- Sexual Harassment of a Minor (Sections 11 & 12):
  * Uttering sexually explicit words, making sounds, or displaying objects with sexual intent to a child.
  * Stalking or constantly watching a child physically, near school/home, or online.
  * Inducing or forcing a child to exhibit their body.
- Child Sexual Abuse Material / Pornography (CSAM) (Sections 13–15):
  * Using a child for pornographic representation (real, simulated, digital, or AI-generated).
  * Storage, possession, transmission, streaming, or commercial distribution of CSAM material.
