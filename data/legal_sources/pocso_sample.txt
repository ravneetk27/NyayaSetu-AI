THE PROTECTION OF CHILDREN FROM SEXUAL OFFENCES (POCSO) ACT, 2012

Section 19: Reporting of offences
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, any person (including the child), who has apprehension that an offence under this Act has been committed or is likely to be committed, shall provide information about the commission of such offence to:
(a) the Special Juvenile Police Unit; or
(b) the local police.
(2) Every report given under sub-section (1) shall be:
(a) ascribed an entry number and recorded in writing;
(b) read over to the informant;
(c) shall be entered in a book to be kept by the Police Station.
(3) Where the report under sub-section (1) is given by a child, the same shall be recorded under sub-section (2) in simple language which can be understood by the child.
(4) Any person who fails to report the commission of an offence as required under sub-section (1) shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to six months or with fine or with both.
